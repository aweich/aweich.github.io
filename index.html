About

I am a computational biologist interested in addressing biological questions through data-driven approaches, combining diverse omics technologies with statistical modelling and computational methods. My research experience spans both academic and industry settings.

My scientific interests lie at the intersection of cancer biology, immunology and cellular immunotherapies. Technically, I work with short- and long-read sequencing, single-cell data analysis, multi-omics integration and network biology.

I am currently pursuing my PhD, where I develop computational and long-read sequencing approaches for high-resolution profiling of CAR T cells, investigating the genomic and epigenomic determinants of their persistence and safety.
